How I got here

Two threads, one person. Engineering dots in vermilion, music in amber.

    Computer science in Hyderabad

    B.Tech at Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University.

  2. Dec 2020

    A first job before graduating

    Software engineer at AppEnsure, an end-user application-performance-monitoring company.

  3. Jun – Dec 2021

    React, on a CRM

    At CloudJune I built CRM dashboards and business workflows in ReactJS and wired them to backend APIs.

  4. 2021 – 2023

    Payments

    Two years at CriticalRiver on payment products: a cross-platform app for contactless payments on smartphones, merchant and distributor portals with role-based access and transaction analytics, and Node.js APIs built to PCI guidelines.

  5. 2023 – 2025

    Investments
